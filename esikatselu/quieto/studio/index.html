Studio

Hello, I'm Ville.

Studio Quieto is a one-person studio in Helsinki. Quieto means quiet in Italian and Portuguese: calm, certain, never loud.

Why

I've always been drawn to two things: beautiful things, and how they work.

A website is where they meet. A good site is beautiful, but it also has to work: say quickly what you do, and get the right person to get in touch.

Still, many businesses do excellent work and look unfinished online. That bothers me, because your website is where people decide whether to trust you. So I make ordinary businesses look the way their work deserves.

Values

Four things that decide everything else.

  1. 01

    Authenticity

    Doing our own thing, with pride. No copying, no invented numbers, and concepts are always marked as concepts.

  2. 02

    Aesthetics

    Every detail considered. The work, the posts and even the emails look like they come from the same studio.

  3. 03

    Freedom

    For you and for the studio. No monthly fees, no lock-in, and the site stays in your name.

  4. 04

    Pride in the work

    The work proves it, not the pitch. That is why the draft comes before any price talk.
