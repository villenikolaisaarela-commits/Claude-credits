Hello, I'm Ville.

Studio Quieto is a one-person studio in Helsinki. Quieto means quiet in Italian and Portuguese: calm, certain, never loud.

WhyRead slowly

I've always been drawn to two things: beautiful things, and how they work. Many businesses do excellent work and look unfinished online. That bothers me. So I make them look the way their work deserves.

Values(04)

Four values. Four characters.

The studio has four values, and each one has a colour and a character. When a decision is hard, one of them usually knows the answer.

  1. 01 · Authenticity · Aito is Finnish for genuine

    Aito

    Says what it is. No copying, no invented numbers, and concepts are marked as concepts.

  2. 02 · Aesthetics · Muoto is Finnish for form

    Muoto

    Measures every detail at arm’s length until it is right. The work, the posts and the emails look like one studio.

  3. 03 · Freedom · Vapaa is Finnish for free

    Vapaa

    No monthly fees, no lock-in. The site and the domain stay in your name.

  4. 04 · Pride · Ylvä is Finnish for proud, noble

    Ylvä

    Shows the work before talking about it. That is why the draft comes first.
