Questions(05)

Asked before every project.

01What does the draft cost?

Nothing. I build a draft of your homepage with your own work, and only then do you decide whether we continue.

02How long does a project take?

Usually two to four weeks from the first call to launch.

03Is VAT added to the prices?

No. The prices are what you pay.

04Who owns the site and the domain?

You do. The site and the domain stay in your name, with no monthly fees.

05What about changes after launch?

You get 30 days of support after launch. After that, changes are done at an hourly rate or a fixed price we agree on.
