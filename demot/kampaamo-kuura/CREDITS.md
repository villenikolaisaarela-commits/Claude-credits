# Credits — Kampaamo Kuura (concept site)

Kampaamo Kuura is a fictional business. All names, prices and client quotes on the site are invented for this portfolio concept. The site intentionally contains no contact details or booking.

## Photos

All photos are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). They were found through the [Openverse API](https://api.openverse.org/) with the filter `license=cc0,pdm`. All files were resized and recompressed (progressive JPEG, quality about 80) and given the same gentle warm, slightly desaturated tonal grade (lifted blacks, warm highlights) so the set reads as one series. ## Fonts

The fonts are self-hosted in `fonts/` (latin subset, WOFF2, taken from Google Fonts), so the page makes no third-party requests. Both families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and the license text is in `fonts/OFL.txt`.

- **Cormorant Garamond** (variable roman and variable italic, weights 300–700) — Copyright 2015 the Cormorant Project Authors (https://github.com/CatharsisFonts/Cormorant)
- **Instrument Sans** (variable, weights 400–700) — Copyright 2022 The Instrument Sans Project Authors (https://github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans)
