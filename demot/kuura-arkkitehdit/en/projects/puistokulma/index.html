A 1960s office building that had stood empty for six years found a new use once two light wells were cut through its floors.

Keeping the frame

The zoning plan would have allowed demolition, but a condition survey showed that the concrete frame was in good shape. We kept the frame and removed only the lightweight partitions and suspended ceilings.

Openings were cut through the floors in two places, bringing daylight into the middle of the deep plan. Galleries and meeting rooms now surround them.

Keeping the frame reduced the carbon footprint of the project by an estimated 1,400 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent compared with demolition and new build.
