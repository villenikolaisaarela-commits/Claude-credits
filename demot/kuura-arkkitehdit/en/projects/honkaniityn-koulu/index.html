Honkaniitty School and Day-care Centre

School and day-care centreVantaaUnder construction, completion 2027

Project facts

Client
City of Vantaa
Location
Vantaa, Finland
Size
6,300 m²
Users
480 pupils (grades 1–6) and 140 children in day care
Status
Under construction, opening August 2027
Procurement
Invited competition 2023, first prize
Delivery
Alliance contract
Structure
CLT walls and floors, glulam frame, concrete ground slab

A two-storey timber school and day-care centre wraps around an old pine grove. The woods stay in the middle of the yard, and every classroom looks out into the treetops.

Competition

The city held an invited competition for the school in 2023. The site includes a pine grove of about one hectare, and the brief allowed it to be cleared for the schoolyard. Our entry kept the woods and placed the building along their edges.

This shelters the yard from the street, and every teaching space opens in two directions: towards the neighbourhood and into the woods.

Home bases

Teaching spaces are grouped into six home bases. Each has three classrooms, a shared learning space and its own entrance from the yard. The youngest pupils and the day-care centre are on the ground floor, the older pupils upstairs.

The dining hall, sports hall and library sit around the main entrance. In the evenings they can be opened to local residents while the rest of the school stays closed.

Timber structure

Walls and floors are cross-laminated timber (CLT), and the sports hall roof rests on 24-metre glulam beams. Timber surfaces are left exposed in classrooms and corridors wherever fire safety allows.

The timber frame was chosen on the basis of a calculation made as early as the competition stage. Construction-phase emissions are about 30 per cent lower than for a comparable concrete-framed school.

On site

Construction began in March 2026. The CLT panels are prefabricated with their window openings, and the frame of each floor goes up in about three weeks. The pine grove was fenced off before work began, and no machinery enters the woods.

The school opens in August 2027.

Key figures

  • 620pupils and children in day care
  • −30%construction-phase emissions compared with a concrete-framed school
  • 1,300 tCO₂ stored in the timber structure

The emissions comparison and carbon storage figures are Kuura’s calculations based on the sketch-design building information model.
