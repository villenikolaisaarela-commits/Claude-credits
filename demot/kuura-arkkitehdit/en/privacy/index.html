Privacy

This website uses no cookies and no visitor tracking. Below we explain how we handle information we receive by email.

Controller

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, visitor tracking or third-party embeds. Fonts are served from our own server.

Email

When you email us, we use your contact details only to reply and, if a project follows, to manage it. We delete messages when they are no longer needed, and no later than three years after our last contact. Correspondence on completed projects is kept in our project archive for the designer’s liability period.

Job applications

We keep applications and portfolios for two years unless the applicant asks us to delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. You can also lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.
