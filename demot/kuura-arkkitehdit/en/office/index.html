Office

Kuura is an architecture office in Helsinki, founded in 2014 by the architects SAFA Maija Rautio and Joel Hakala. We are a team of twelve.

Photography: Antoine Gravier and Ryan Ancill / Unsplash

Office

We design housing, schools and day-care centres, as well as alterations to existing buildings. Most of our projects are either built in timber or give an old building a new use.

History

In 2014 the office was a 34-square-metre room in Sörnäinen. The first big table was two old doors that Joel Hakala bought in Kallio for twenty euros. The doors are still in use as the model-making bench. We moved to Hämäränkatu in 2018, when the sixth desk was already in the hallway.

Principles

Site first

We read the site before we draw. Trees, rock, the direction of light and the neighbours decide where a building sits and where its windows open.

Structure on show

We use few materials and let them show. When the structure is also the finish, a building is easier to maintain and repair.

Long service life

We size spaces so that their use can change. The lowest-carbon building is the one that does not have to be demolished in thirty years.

Services

  • Architectural designFrom sketches to building permit and technical design.
  • Lead designWe take responsibility for the design as a whole and coordinate the design team, including on large public projects.
  • Interior architectureFitted furniture, materials and lighting drawn together with the building.
  • Renovation and conversionFrom building history surveys to changes of use.
  • Project planningNeeds assessments and room programmes before the decision to go ahead is made.
  • Architecture competitionsWe enter two or three competitions a year, open and invited.

How we work

  1. Needs assessmentWe go through the site, needs, schedule and budget. The result is a room programme and an estimate of the project size.2–4 weeks
  2. Sketch designWe develop two or three alternative schemes and refine one of them.1–3 months
  3. Building permitWe prepare the permit drawings and handle the process with the building control authority.2–4 months
  4. Technical designWorking drawings, details and coordination with the structural and building services engineers.2–6 months
  5. ConstructionWe take part in site meetings and refine the drawings throughout construction.during construction

Durations are typical for a private house. Public projects take longer.
