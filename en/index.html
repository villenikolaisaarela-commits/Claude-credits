Frequently asked

How is this different from a normal agency?

A normal agency builds the site, sends the invoice and moves on to the next project. We stay: we maintain the site, make the changes and improve it every month so that it brings in work. And you see a demo before you pay anything.

Do I have to update the site myself?

No. Just tell us what you want changed: by message, photo or phone call. You never have to log in anywhere or learn a content management system.

How fast do you reply?

Within one working day, always. Small changes are live within two working days, and bigger ones go into the monthly update.

Do I have time for this between jobs?

Yes. A link and a few sentences are all we need. We make the demo, and you can look at it on your phone in the evening.

Is the demo just a picture?

No. The demo is a working front page at its own link. You can open it on your phone and computer and show it to your colleagues.

What does the demo cost?

Nothing. The demo is free and comes with no commitment.

What does the site cost?

€199 a month + VAT, with no setup fee. You can also pay for a year at once, €1,990 + VAT, which gives you two months free. The price covers design, build, maintenance, changes and updates.

Why a monthly price and not a one-off fee?

A site is not finished on launch day. With a monthly price it stays up to date and keeps improving: new projects, services and fixes are done without separate quotes, and you do not have to pay thousands of euros at once.

What if I want to stop?

The minimum term is 12 months, and after that you can end the contract with one month’s notice. The domain is always yours. You can buy the site outright for €990, and after 24 months it is transferred to you free.

How does the quote request guarantee work?

If the site’s quote request form brings in no quote requests in the first three months after launch, the next three months are free. The tracking lets you see for yourself what comes through the form.

How long does the project take?

Usually two to four weeks. The demo is ready in 48 hours, and the schedule is agreed before we start.

We do not have good photos.

Photos taken on a phone are fine to start with. We edit them so your work looks on the site the way it does in real life.

What happens after launch?

We make small changes within two working days, and once a month we do a bigger update, such as a new project or service page. Every month you get a short report: how many enquiries came in, from where, and what to do next.

What do I actually get?

A finished site at your own address with ongoing maintenance and improvement: design, copy, photo editing, a site that works on a phone, findability and a tuned-up Google Business Profile. The domain is always yours.

Who will I be working with?

Ville Saarela. He designs, writes and builds the sites himself, with no middlemen, and replies to every message himself.

Are the projects real clients?

Some of the work is concept work: fictional companies that show what a site in the trade can do. This is stated on every project page.
